我們如何處理資料

本遊戲由個人開發者方士軒（Shih-Hsuan Fang）提供，沿用識別碼 tw.mars.villagesiege，整合《村莊攻防》與《無盡塔防》的玩法。

遊戲不要求帳號，不內建廣告、分析或跨 App 追蹤工具，也不將遊戲進度、裝置識別碼或使用紀錄傳給開發者。遊戲不要求相機、麥克風、通訊錄或定位權限。

章節、結晶、設定、中途戰局與自願匯入的舊作紀錄，會存於裝置的本機儲存空間。匯入需先預覽再確認；匯出的 JSON 備份由你自行保管，不會自動上傳。遊戲沒有開發者提供的雲端存檔或跨裝置同步服務。

你可以透過遊戲的存檔功能匯出備份。清除 App／網站資料或移除 App 可能刪除本機進度，請先備份；開發者無法從伺服器還原未備份的存檔。系統層級的備份與商店診斷依你與平台的設定處理。

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026.

Trine Frontier is provided by individual developer Shih-Hsuan Fang (方士軒). It retains the identifier tw.mars.villagesiege and combines Village Siege and Endless Tower Defense into one game.

The game requires no account. It includes no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking tools, and sends no game progress, device identifiers or activity records to the developer. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

Local progress and backups

Chapters, crystals, settings, unfinished battles and voluntarily imported legacy records are stored locally on your device. Imports require preview and confirmation. Exported JSON backups remain under your control and are not automatically uploaded. The developer provides no cloud-save or cross-device synchronization service.

Use the game's save controls to export a backup before clearing app or website data or uninstalling. These actions may erase progress. The developer cannot recover an unbacked-up save from a server. Operating-system backups and store diagnostics follow your platform settings.

Support correspondence

If you email a820628a@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any screenshots or backups you choose to attach, to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal information. Email is processed by the email provider. You may request deletion of support correspondence, except information that must be retained by law.

Website and platform services

When you visit this page or the web version, the hosting provider may process your IP address and access logs to serve the site, under its own policy. This policy does not replace the policies of Apple, Google, your operating system or your email provider.

Children and updates

This game contains fantasy combat and is not an educational app designed specifically for children. The developer does not actively collect children's personal information through the game. Changes to data handling will be reflected here and in the store disclosures. Contact the email above for privacy questions.
